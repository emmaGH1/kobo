# Kobo Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 4 November 2026

## Request deletion

Email **emma080355@gmail.com** with the subject **Kobo data deletion request**.

Include only enough information to locate your records:

- Whether you used WhatsApp, owner web access or the public demo.
- For WhatsApp, the phone number you used, including its country code. Share it privately by email, not on a public page.
- Approximately when you used Kobo and whether you want particular information or all associated information reviewed for deletion.
- If you used the web demo, an approximate session time and a non-sensitive description that may help locate the session. Do not send session cookies or tokens. Anonymous demo sessions may be difficult to identify after their cookies expire.

Do not include your owner passcode, API keys, WhatsApp verification codes, banking details or unrelated customer records.

## What happens next

The Kobo operator will review the request and may ask for proportionate verification of your identity and authority over the records. You cannot authorize deletion of another trader's records merely because they mention you; we will assess such requests separately.

Relevant information may include submitted text, voice transcripts, extracted notebook text, customer information, pending reviews, financial records, preferences, session records and WhatsApp processing metadata.

**Deletion is manual, not an automated feature.** Confirmed ledger records are append-only in normal operation, and a reversal corrects a balance without erasing the original entry. A deletion request therefore needs a separate technical and legal assessment. We will explain the action taken, any information that must be retained and the reason for retaining it. We do not promise instant deletion or a fixed completion or backup-expiry period; applicable legal requirements still apply.

If identifiable backup copies exist, their handling must also be assessed. Do not assume that deleting active records immediately removes every historical copy.

## What does not delete Kobo's records

Deleting your WhatsApp conversation, clearing browser cookies, signing out, starting a new demo or cancelling a pending review does not by itself erase server-side records.

Temporary voice files are normally removed after processing, but transcripts may remain. Notebook images are not persisted by the image-reading path, but extracted records may remain.

## Copies held by other providers

Kobo cannot directly erase copies independently held by WhatsApp/Meta, a model API provider or your own device. Their privacy controls and request procedures apply to those copies. Contact us if you need help understanding which service processed your information.
